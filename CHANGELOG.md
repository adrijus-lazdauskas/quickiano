## [0.1.0](https://github.com/adrijus-lazdauskas/quickiano/tree/0.1.0) - 2026-10-19

### 🚀 Features

- *(score)* Parse musicxml and mxl into the score model
- *(fingering)* Suggest fingerings for both hands
- *(notes)* Name notes in english, german or solfege
- *(audio)* Play salamander piano samples with a synth fallback
- *(player)* Schedule playback with loop, count-in and live tempo
- *(demos)* Add für elise and ode to joy as public domain demos
- *(keyboard)* Draw a keyboard fitted to the song
- *(falling)* Show falling notes with glow, sparks and smoke
- *(sheet)* Show sheet music with fingering and note names
- *(import)* Open midi files
- *(settings)* Keep view, note names, hand colors and effects
- *(library)* Remember songs, bar ranges and fingers in the browser
- *(ui)* Add the start screen, top bar, timeline and control bar
